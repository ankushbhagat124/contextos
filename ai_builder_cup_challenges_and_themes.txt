CHALLENGE 1: BFSI: Intelligent Risk, Fraud & Financial Experiences
------------------------------------------------------------------
• Problem Statement:
  Build an AI-powered solution that addresses meaningful challenges across banking, financial services, insurance, or related industries. Solutions may focus on detecting and mitigating fraud or risk, automating complex processes, improving customer interactions, or delivering more personalized and informed financial experiences.
• Suggested Solution Directions:
  - Fraud detection
  - Transaction anomaly analysis
  - Document intelligence
  - Financial assistance / AI financial advisors
  - Personalized recommendations
  - Compliance automation
  - Risk assessment & credit scoring
  - Claims processing automation
• Suggested Google Cloud Technologies:
  - Gemini on Vertex AI for reasoning and conversational experiences
  - BigQuery for transaction and data analytics
  - Cloud Storage
  - Cloud Run
  - Pub/Sub
  - Vertex AI Search & Conversation

[1.4] PROTOTYPE SUBMISSION REQUIREMENTS & FORM FIELDS
(Extracted from Hack2skill Submissions Module & Interactive Submission Modal)

Mandatory Submission Rules:
1. Pitch Deck (PDF):
   - Participants must submit their pitch deck using the prescribed template provided for the challenge.
   - Prescribed Presentation Template:
     https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/13rg7vW43mEH6DkpuusAE6fdoEylSFz8waNpE4RLzoUg/edit?slide=id.p#slide=id.p/export/pptx
   - Converted & uploaded as PDF (Maximum file size: 5 MB).
   - Submissions not following the required format may be subject to disqualification.
2. Video Demonstration Link:
   - Participants must submit a 3 to 4 minute video demonstration of their solution.
   - Must clearly demonstrate working functionality of the submitted solution.
   - Publicly accessible link on YouTube, Vimeo, or Google Drive.
   - Videos exceeding the prescribed duration may attract a penalty or result in disqualification from advancing to prototype evaluation.
3. Deployed Prototype Link:
   - Participants must provide a valid and accessible link to a deployed, working prototype.
   - Must be deployed on Google Cloud Platform (Cloud Run, GCP, or Firebase).
   - The prototype must remain functional throughout the evaluation process.
   - If the application, APIs, integrations, or deployment components are unavailable or non-functional during evaluation, no additional consideration, retries, or allowances will be provided.
4. GitHub Repository Link:
   - Participants must provide an accessible, public GitHub repository containing all relevant source code and supporting documentation.
   - Must remain publicly accessible throughout the entire evaluation process.
5. Brief Description:
   - Text explanation (Maximum 1024 characters) detailing what was built.
   - Must explicitly describe how Google Cloud/Firebase services are leveraged (e.g., Gemini, Cloud Run, Firestore, Vertex AI).
6. Challenge Category:
   - Selection of the corresponding theme category from the dropdown menu.

Submission Deadline:
- October 18, 2026 at 11:59 PM (IST)

[2.2] WHAT YOU'LL BUILD (PROTOTYPE SPECIFICATION)
"A working prototype that addresses your chosen problem statement. The goal is a functional solution that demonstrates a clear idea in action—not just a pitch deck or a mockup. Crucially, all submissions must leverage Google AI models (like Gemini or Gemma) or be built using agentic platforms (such as Agent Platform, Antigravity, or AI Studio), and they must be deployed on Google Cloud via Cloud Run or Firebase.
We'll be hosting online sessions covering the overall program and a walkthrough of the problem statements, so you can attend for clarity as you build."

Key Technical Mandates:
• Core AI Models / Platforms:
  - Google AI models: Family of Gemini or Gemma models
  - Agentic platforms: Agent Platform, Google Antigravity, or Google AI Studio
• Deployment & Infrastructure:
  - Must be deployed on Google Cloud Platform using Cloud Run or Firebase.


[2.3] WHAT YOU'LL SUBMIT
Following requirements are mandatory while submitting your solution:
1. Clear Proposal:
   - Submit a compelling vision for your AI solution, highlighting its positive impact and alignment with one of the problem statements in a deck/PPT converted into a PDF.
   - Clearly articulate the problem your solution addresses and how it will benefit the community.
   - Demonstrate the feasibility of your project and its potential for scalability.
2. Functional Prototype:
   - Build a working prototype using Google Cloud's AI tools & deployed on Cloud Run / GCP / Firebase.
   - Ensure the prototype effectively demonstrates your solution's capabilities and benefits.
3. Comprehensive Documentation:
   - Include a link to a public YouTube or Vimeo video or public Google Drive link (3 minutes) showcasing your AI creation in action.
4. Category Specification:
   - Clearly identify the problem statement you are submitting for.


[2.4] SUBMISSION SPECIFICS
• Functionality:
  - Your project must be functional and demonstrate a clear solution to the chosen challenge.
  - The solution should be user-friendly, accessible, and scalable.
  - Ensure that your project aligns with the hackathon's focus on impactful solutions.
• Platform Compatibility:
  - Your solution should be compatible with the specified Google Cloud platform and tools.
  - Consider factors like scalability, performance, and integration with existing systems.
• Language:
  - All submission materials, including code, documentation, and presentations, must be in English.


[2.5] JUDGING CRITERIA (WEIGHTED SCORING BREAKDOWN)
(Extracted from Interactive Criteria Accordion Toggles)

1. Technical Merit & Gen AI Implementation — 40%
   • Meaningful use of Gen AI:
     - How effectively and meaningfully has Generative AI been incorporated into the solution?
     - Does the use of Gen AI contribute directly to solving the problem or enhancing the overall solution?
   • Technical implementation:
     - Is the solution functional, technically sound, and appropriately implemented?
   • Scalability & sustainability:
     - Does the solution demonstrate potential for wider adoption, scalability, and future development beyond the prototype stage?

2. Problem Alignment & Impact — 25%
   • Problem alignment:
     - How effectively does the solution address the chosen problem statement, challenge objective, or theme?
   • Potential impact:
     - Does the solution demonstrate clear and meaningful value for its intended users, organization, industry, community, or other relevant stakeholders?

3. Innovation & Creativity — 25%
   • Originality & differentiation:
     - Does the solution demonstrate a fresh perspective, novel approach, or meaningful differentiation from existing solutions?
   • Innovation in the use of Gen AI:
     - Does the solution demonstrate a creative or innovative application of Generative AI beyond a basic or superficial implementation?

4. User Experience & Solution Design — 10%
   • Usability & accessibility:
     - Is the solution intuitive, user-friendly, and reasonably accessible to its intended users?
   • Experience & integration:
     - Do the various components, including the Gen AI capabilities, work together cohesively to deliver a smooth and meaningful user experience?
